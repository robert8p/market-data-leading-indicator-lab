It independently specifies exact-calendar split-only price returns, annualized sample standard deviation of 20 simple returns including the current session, preceding-20-session volume z-score, and absolute one-session adjusted return divided by positive regular-session dollar volume. It defines regular-session close/volume/dollar-volume using native minute observations. The retained source's original historical scope is preserved; it is not a claim that new target inputs meet those qualifications.

The following is a **description of the current implementation**, provided to expose assumptions for independent review. It is not a newly frozen authority and must not substitute for independent formula verification:

- Each `split_factor` is the effective session's new-shares/old-shares ratio. The cumulative product through the session multiplies price and divides volume. These are split-only returns, not dividend or spin-off total returns.
- The four raw and adjusted return array entries use exact 1/5/20/60 session lags. Every close in the inclusive horizon must be valid; source count must equal one and close must be positive. Missing history remains NULL. Adjusted horizons additionally require no unresolved action in the intervening sessions. Multiple distinct non-NULL identity anchors block a horizon; unresolved identity and mixed source basis remain quality flags and do not constitute accepted continuity.
- `realized_log_vol_20d` is unannualized sample standard deviation of the 20 adjusted one-session log returns ending at the current session. Its annualized variant multiplies by sqrt(252). `annualized_simple_vol_20d` uses the analogous simple returns. All require 21 valid closes and 20 returns, no unresolved actions and no differing known identity anchors.
- Relative volume and volume z-score compare current split-normalized volume with the preceding 20 sessions, excluding current. Mean must be positive for relative volume; sample standard deviation must be positive for the z-score. A genuinely source-published current volume of zero can produce relative volume zero when the full reference window and positive denominator exist. Missing volume never becomes zero.
- `daily_amihud` is absolute adjusted one-session simple return divided by positive source dollar volume. `amihud_mean_20d` is the arithmetic mean of 20 such values ending at current, requiring all 20 plus the corresponding 21-close window.
- `input_coverage` contains four inclusive close counts for horizons 1/5/20/60, then the 20-log-return count, prior-20 adjusted-volume count, current-inclusive 20-Amihud count, and a current valid-volume indicator.
